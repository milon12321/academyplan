Privacy Notice

We collect only information you submit to this website or that is needed to operate your account, such as your Firebase user ID, display name, email address when provided, account timestamps, study-plan metadata, and your study content.

An authorized owner may access this website data only to operate, support, secure, and improve the service. Administrative access is restricted by Firebase authentication and server-side administrator authorization. Access may be recorded in an audit log.

The owner cannot see your Gmail password, private Gmail contents, Google Drive files, private activity outside this website, or data you did not submit here. Data is retained while needed to provide the service and may be deleted on a valid request, subject to legal or security retention requirements.

Google sign-in does not share your password with this site. For export, deletion, or privacy questions, contact the site owner at milonsarkar1231@gmail.com.
